Our Roots

Famous in Indupalli Village

Srinivasa Cooking & Caterings are renowned for excellence in Indupalli village near Vijayawada, Krishna District. For years, we have been the first choice for wedding auspicious events and grand celebrations.

Our journey in Cooking & Catering by Indupalli is built on a foundation of authentic tradition and elite service. We bring the rich culinary heritage of the Krishna district to every plate we serve, ensuring your special moments are truly auspicious.

From humble beginnings serving our local community, we have grown into a trusted name across Hyderabad and Ranga Reddy district — delivering freshly prepared, hygienically maintained, and lovingly cooked food for every type of celebration.
